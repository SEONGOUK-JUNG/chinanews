Frequently asked questions

How do you verify 6N purity?
By GDMS (glow-discharge mass spectrometry) covering the full periodic table, not a 10-element ICP sum. Buyers may nominate an independent lab; we will not quote against certificates that exclude key impurities (Ag, S, O, As, Sb, Bi).
Do you supply OFC / C10100?
Yes — OFE/C10100 rod, bar and plate from Chinese mills, with oxygen ≤ 5 ppm certificates.
Minimum order?
6N: 10 kg; 5N: 25 kg; cathode/OFC: 1 t. Samples 100–500 g.
