Ethylene Glycol (MEG) — China Spot Price

As of 2026-11-11 · 글리콜

What ethylene glycol is

Monoethylene glycol (MEG) is a raw material for polyester. Most of it reacts with PTA to make polyester fibre for clothing and PET bottle resin; some is used in antifreeze.

China's market

China is the world's largest MEG consumer. Supply comes from oil-based (ethylene) plants, China's distinctive coal-based MEG plants and imports from the Middle East and elsewhere. Port inventories in East China (Zhangjiagang and nearby) are a key price signal, and MEG futures trade on the Dalian Commodity Exchange.

What moves the price

  1. Polyester run rates — demand from fibre and bottle-resin plants
  2. Port inventories — stock changes in East China
  3. Feedstock — crude oil (ethylene) and coal
  4. Imports — cargoes from the Middle East and North America
  5. Futures — moves in Dalian MEG futures
