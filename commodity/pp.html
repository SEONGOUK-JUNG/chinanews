Polypropylene (PP) — China Spot Price

As of 2026-11-06 · 폴리프로필렌

What polypropylene is

Polypropylene (PP) is one of the most widely used plastics: food containers and packaging film, car bumpers and interior parts, appliance parts, nonwovens and fibres. It comes in injection, film and fibre grades, among others.

China's market

China is the world's largest producer and consumer of PP. Its propylene feedstock comes from three routes:

  • Oil-based — naphtha cracking
  • Propane dehydrogenation (PDH) — using imported propane
  • Coal-based — coal-to-olefins

Large capacity has been added in recent years, and PP futures trade on the Dalian Commodity Exchange.

What moves the price

  1. Feedstock — crude oil, propane and coal
  2. New capacity — start-ups add supply
  3. Downstream demand — packaging, autos and appliances
  4. Inventory — producer and trader stocks
  5. Futures — moves in Dalian PP futures
