Propylene Oxide (PO) — China Spot Price

As of 2026-10-23 · 프로필렌 옥사이드

What propylene oxide is

Propylene oxide (PO) is a chemical intermediate made from propylene. Most of it becomes polyether polyols and then polyurethane foam for furniture, car seats and insulation; the rest goes into propylene glycol and surfactants.

China's market

China is the world's largest PO producer. The wastewater-heavy chlorohydrin route used to dominate, but capacity using HPPO (hydrogen peroxide) and co-oxidation routes that also make styrene or MTBE has grown quickly. New plants keep adding supply.

What moves the price

  1. Propylene price — the feedstock cost
  2. Polyether demand — furniture, auto and construction activity
  3. New capacity — large start-ups weigh on prices
  4. Process rules — the phase-out of chlorohydrin plants
  5. Co-product prices — co-oxidation plants also depend on styrene and MTBE margins
