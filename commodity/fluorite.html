Fluorite (Fluorspar) — China Spot Price

As of 2026-11-09 · 형석

What fluorite is

Fluorite (fluorspar, CaF₂) is the feedstock of fluorine chemistry. Its use depends on grade.

TypeGradeMain uses
Acid gradeCaF₂ ≥ 97%Hydrofluoric acid → refrigerants, fluoropolymers, battery electrolyte (LiPF₆), semiconductor etchants
Metallurgical gradeCaF₂ about 65–90%Flux in iron and steel making

China's market

China is the world's largest producer and consumer of fluorite, with mines in Zhejiang, Jiangxi, Inner Mongolia and Hunan. As domestic fluorochemical demand has grown, imports from Mongolia and elsewhere have risen. Several countries classify fluorspar as a strategic or critical mineral.

What moves the price

  1. Hydrofluoric acid demand — refrigerants, fluoropolymers and battery electrolyte
  2. Refrigerant quotas — production quotas under greenhouse-gas commitments
  3. Mine safety and environmental inspections — supply drops while they run
  4. Imports — concentrate from Mongolia and other sources
  5. Seasonality — lower winter output at northern mines
