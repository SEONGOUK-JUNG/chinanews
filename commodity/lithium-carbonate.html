Lithium Carbonate — China Spot Price

As of 2026-10-16 · 탄산리튬

What lithium carbonate is

Lithium carbonate (Li₂CO₃) is the most widely traded lithium compound. Its largest use is in battery cathode materials for electric vehicles and energy storage systems; it is also used in glass and ceramics, lubricating greases and pharmaceuticals.

GradePurityMain uses
Battery grade≥ 99.5%LFP and NCM cathode materials
Industrial gradeabout 99.0–99.2%Glass and ceramics, greases, chemicals

Why China sets the price

China refines roughly two-thirds of the world's lithium chemicals. Its feedstock comes from three directions:

  • Yichun, Jiangxi — lepidolite mines and refineries
  • Qinghai and Tibet — lithium extracted from salt-lake brines
  • Imports — spodumene concentrate from Australia and raw material from Chile and Argentina

China's domestic spot price therefore works as the de facto global benchmark. Since 2023 lithium carbonate futures have traded on the Guangzhou Futures Exchange, and spot and futures prices move each other.

What moves the price

  1. EV and energy-storage demand — above all domestic sales of LFP batteries in China
  2. Feedstock cost — spodumene concentrate prices and brine production costs
  3. Supply discipline — permits and environmental inspections at Yichun mines
  4. Inventory — restocking and destocking by refiners and battery makers
  5. Futures — sharp moves in Guangzhou futures feed straight into spot offers
