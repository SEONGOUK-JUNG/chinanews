Polysilicon — China Spot Price

As of 2026-10-29 · 폴리실리콘

What polysilicon is

Polysilicon is ultra-pure polycrystalline silicon, the starting material for solar wafers and semiconductor wafers. The required purity differs greatly by use.

TypePurityUse
Solar gradeabout 6N–9NMono ingots and wafers for solar cells
Electronic gradeabout 11NSingle-crystal silicon wafers for chips

It is made mainly as rods by the Siemens process or as granules in fluidised-bed reactors.

China's market

Most of the world's polysilicon is produced in China, in large plants in Xinjiang, Inner Mongolia, Sichuan and Yunnan where power is cheap. The downstream solar chain — ingots, wafers, cells and modules — is concentrated there too. Polysilicon futures were listed on the Guangzhou Futures Exchange at the end of 2024.

What moves the price

  1. Solar installations — in China and abroad
  2. Overcapacity — capacity well above demand after rapid expansion, and moves to cut output and consolidate
  3. Power costs — electricity is a large share of production cost
  4. Wafer and module prices — falling downstream prices press on feedstock
  5. Trade measures — import restrictions on solar products in major markets
