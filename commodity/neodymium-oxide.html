Neodymium Oxide — China Spot Price

As of 2026-11-09 · 산화네오디뮴

What neodymium oxide is

Neodymium oxide (Nd₂O₃) is the key light rare-earth oxide and the core raw material for NdFeB magnets, the strongest permanent magnets. They go into EV traction motors, wind turbines, appliance motors, phone components and industrial robots. It is also widely traded blended with praseodymium as PrNd oxide.

China's market

China accounts for most rare-earth mining and roughly 90% of separation and refining. Inner Mongolia (light rare earths) and Jiangxi and Guangdong (ionic-clay deposits) are the main sources, and feedstock is also imported from Myanmar. The government sets total mining and smelting quotas and applies export controls to some rare earths and related technologies.

What moves the price

  1. Magnet demand — EVs, wind power and appliances
  2. Production quotas — mining and smelting allocations
  3. Export controls — licensing and changes in covered items
  4. Feedstock imports — from Myanmar and elsewhere
  5. Industry consolidation — output managed by large rare-earth groups
