Pure Benzene — China Spot Price

As of 2026-11-10 · 순수 벤젠

What benzene is

Benzene is the main aromatic building block, used almost entirely to make other chemicals:

  • Styrene (SM) → PS and ABS resins
  • Phenol → bisphenol A and polycarbonate
  • Caprolactam → nylon
  • Aniline → MDI for polyurethane

China's market

China is the world's largest benzene consumer. It uses petroleum benzene from refineries and crackers alongside benzene refined from coke-oven by-products, and imports the shortfall — South Korea is one of China's main benzene suppliers. Port inventories in East China are a key price signal.

What moves the price

  1. Crude oil
  2. Downstream margins — especially styrene
  3. Port inventories — stock changes in East China
  4. Imports — cargoes from Korea and elsewhere
  5. Refinery and cracker run rates
