Coal — China Spot Price

As of 2026-11-04 · 석탄

What coal is

Coal splits into two main markets: thermal coal burned in power plants and coking coal turned into coke for iron making. Thermal coal is usually priced by calorific value (for example 5,500 kcal/kg).

China's market

China accounts for about half of the world's coal production and consumption. Shanxi, Inner Mongolia and Shaanxi are the three main producing regions, and China also imports from Indonesia, Australia, Mongolia and Russia. Inventories and prices at northern ports such as Qinhuangdao serve as the market benchmark.

What moves the price

  1. Power demand — summer cooling and winter heating peaks
  2. Hydropower output — dry seasons mean more coal-fired generation
  3. Mine safety inspections and output policy
  4. Imports
  5. Port and power-plant stocks
