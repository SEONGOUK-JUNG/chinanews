Hot-Rolled Coil (HRC) — China Spot Price

As of 2026-11-03 · 열간 압연 코일

What hot-rolled coil is

Hot-rolled coil (HRC) is steel sheet rolled from slab at high temperature. It is used for pipes, auto parts, machinery, shipbuilding and construction, and is the base material for cold-rolled and coated sheet. It is the usual benchmark for flat-steel prices.

China's market

China produces more than half of the world's crude steel, with large mills in Hebei, Jiangsu and Shandong. HRC futures trade on the Shanghai Futures Exchange and act as a reference for spot prices. When domestic demand is weak, exports rise and affect steel prices elsewhere.

What moves the price

  1. Raw materials — iron ore and coking coal
  2. Domestic demand — property, infrastructure and manufacturing
  3. Output controls — production cuts and environmental curbs
  4. Exports — export volumes and anti-dumping duties abroad
  5. Futures — moves in Shanghai HRC futures
